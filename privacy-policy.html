Merge Climb – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Developer: GALLUPTIM LLC, Pennsylvania, USA

This policy explains what information the Merge Climb game ("the app") handles. The short version: we do not collect your personal information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads.

Information we collect

We, GALLUPTIM LLC, do not collect, store or receive any personal information from the app. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.

Your game progress (current level, board, score, best score and hints) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. To serve, measure and protect these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance data

This data is sent directly to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's own privacy policy. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): When required, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can change your choice anytime with the Privacy button on the game screen.
  • Personalized ads: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Your game data: Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
